Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tyler Barron handles information for the private personal-assistant messaging application operated for the owner's personal use.

Information processed

  • Mobile phone number
  • SMS messages sent to the assistant
  • Timestamps and delivery metadata
  • Assistant responses
  • Reminder and notification requests
  • Technical logs necessary to operate and secure the service

How information is used

Information is used to provide the personal assistant service, process SMS commands, generate assistant responses, deliver requested reminders and notifications, and maintain reliability, security, and abuse prevention.

Processing and retention

The assistant model and primary application state are hosted locally on infrastructure controlled by the owner. Twilio acts as the SMS transport provider and necessarily processes SMS content and metadata in transit.

Information is retained only as needed for operation and troubleshooting. Credentials are not intentionally transmitted through SMS. Users should not send passwords, authentication codes, API keys, financial credentials, or other highly sensitive secrets through SMS.

SMS consent and marketing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS opt-in and consent data is not sold, rented, or provided to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Twilio's processing as the SMS transport provider is necessary to deliver messages.

SMS opt-out

Reply STOP to stop receiving SMS messages. Reply START to opt in again where supported. Reply HELP for assistance.

The keyword flow is currently documented as planned; see the SMS Opt-In section for its implementation status.
