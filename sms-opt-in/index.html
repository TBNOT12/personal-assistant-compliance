You're subscribed to receive SMS messages from Tyler Barron. Message frequency varies based on your requests, reminders, and assistant notifications. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

The confirmation message above is sent immediately after the authorized owner's START is received in the planned flow.

Enrollment and messaging rules

Under this planned flow:

  • Enrollment is confirmed only after START is received.
  • Messages are sent only to the authorized owner number.
  • No other users are invited to enroll. There is no public user signup.
  • The service provides personal-assistant responses, reminders, and requested notifications. No marketing messages are sent.
  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help.

Neither the owner's personal phone number nor the designated assistant SMS number is published here.
